QuadFight is a free, non-commercial falling-block puzzle game with a CPU that plays like a person, 12 fighters with
special attacks, and local 2-player versus. v1.0.1 is the first release you can download for **Windows, macOS, Linux and the Web**.

## Highlights
- **Now on four platforms:** Windows 10/11 (x64), macOS 11+ (universal: Apple Silicon + Intel), Linux (x86-64) and the Web (also on iOS Safari). New app icon.
- **VS CPU, Lv 1–20:** an opponent that only presses keys, with input lag, thinking time, key repeat, hold (from Lv 4), one-piece lookahead (from Lv 11) and believable mistakes.
- **12 fighters, each with a special attack** that you charge by clearing lines in the right sizes (heroes and animals, pixel-art portraits and effects, a theme song each).
- **LADDER, TOURNAMENT and PRACTICE:** climb one opponent at a time; fight 5 opponents and a "dark" copy of yourself as the boss, with continues and a car-smashing bonus stage; or practise without being recorded.
- **2 PLAYERS:** local versus on one computer (keyboard / two gamepads) or on a tablet (two sets of touch buttons).
- **Skill estimate:** your speed, stacking and reaction are mapped to a CPU level and shown after fights and games.
- **Fight stats:** per-fight and tournament summaries (sent / taken / blocked attacks, best combo, quads, specials, time).
- **Controls everywhere:** keyboard (rebindable, 3 slots per action), gamepad, touch layouts (Classic / Thumbs / gamepad style); phone landscape layout and full-screen button.
- **Options:** 4 block themes, 3 backgrounds, 2 sound styles, 7 original music styles, ghost piece and key-repeat speed.
- **Original everything:** art, music and sound are generated by the Python tools in the repository. No third-party art or audio.

## Installing the unsigned builds
- **Windows:** SmartScreen → **More info** → **Run anyway**.
- **macOS:** right-click the app → **Open**; if it says "damaged", run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/QuadFight.app`.
- **Linux:** `tar xzf QuadFight-v1.0.1-linux-x86_64.tar.gz && ./QuadFight.x86_64` (`chmod +x` if needed).
- Verify downloads with `sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS.txt` (macOS: `shasum -a 256 -c`).

## Known limitations
- Windows and macOS builds are **not code-signed or notarized** (no paid certificates), so the operating system shows a warning on first launch.
- The author builds and tests mainly on Linux and in the browser (iOS Safari is the main web target). The Windows and macOS builds are produced by CI and have had less hands-on testing; please report problems.
- 2 PLAYERS is offered only on computers and tablets (not on phones).
- Linux needs a GPU/driver with OpenGL 3.3. No ARM builds for Windows/Linux yet.
