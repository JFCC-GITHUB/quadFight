## Music and sound
All music is original, composed and synthesized for this project with `tools/gen_music.py` and `tools/gen_themes.py`
(styles: Super Robot, Chiptune, Synthwave, Chill, Lo-fi Cafe, Bossa Breeze, Music Box, plus one theme per fighter) and is covered by the project's MIT License.
The Super Robot style is an homage to the general sound of 1970s–80s Japanese "super robot" anime themes
(heroic minor-key brass, rock drums); it does not reproduce any existing song.
Sound effects are generated in code (`scripts/sfx.gd`). No third-party audio is bundled.
