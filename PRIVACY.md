# QuadFight – Privacy Policy 🇹🇼

**English** · [繁體中文](PRIVACY.zh-TW.md)

_Last updated: 2026-11-28 (v1.0.1)_

QuadFight is a free, non-commercial hobby game. **It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.**

## What the game stores (on your device only)
| Data | Why | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Settings (`settings.cfg`): colours, audio, key bindings, control layout, your per-level win/loss record and skill estimate | So your choices are remembered | Desktop: the Godot user-data folder (Windows `%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\QuadFight`, macOS `~/Library/Application Support/Godot/app_userdata/QuadFight`, Linux `~/.local/share/godot/app_userdata/QuadFight`). Web: the browser's local storage for the site |
| Local leaderboard (`scores.json`): the player name you type, score, lines, level, date | Your ranking screen | Same place |

This data never leaves your device. Delete the folder (or clear the site data in your browser) to erase it.

## What the game does NOT do
- No network connections, accounts, sign-in, analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, ads or in-app purchases.
- No access to your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files outside its own data folder.
- No cookies set by the game. (The web build only uses the browser's local storage, as above.)

## Web version hosted on GitHub Pages
The online version is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the host, may log technical data such as your IP address when you load the page,
under [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The author has no access to those logs and adds no tracking of their own. Downloaded desktop builds make no network requests at all.

## Children
The game collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact
If this policy changes, the new version is published in this repository with an updated date.
Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/JFCC-GITHUB/quadFight/issues>.
